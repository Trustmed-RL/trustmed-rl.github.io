Now be the patient

The recording above is one fixed case. Here the trained model runs live: pick a patient from the test set, answer the doctor's questions yourself, and watch it examine, order tests, check the images and commit to a diagnosis.

The model runs on a shared Hugging Face GPU, and each doctor turn draws on your own free daily allowance there: about 2 minutes without an account, 5 minutes with a free one. A full case needs roughly 2 minutes. Opening it in a new tab lets you sign in to Hugging Face for the larger allowance.

Would you catch it?

In some episodes the environment quietly swaps one returned image for a picture from a different patient. The agent has to notice before it reads anything into it.

This is a real test episode. A chest CT was ordered and two images came back. For each image, say whether it matches the order, then submit your verdict. Click an image to enlarge it.

A right answer is worth what the evidence behind it is worth

The reward for a diagnosis is scaled by how much of the available evidence the agent actually obtained and verified. Guessing early is cheap to try and pays almost nothing.

Drag the slider to see what the same correct diagnosis earns at different levels of evidence.

How it compares

An 8-billion-parameter open model trained with reinforcement learning in this environment, next to its own untrained and supervised-only versions, three larger open models, and two proprietary ones.

Training changes how the agent works a case up

The gain is not only in the final answer. The same 8B model goes from barely investigating to running a full workup: more tests, more verified images, more consults, more searches, and far fewer cases abandoned without a diagnosis.
